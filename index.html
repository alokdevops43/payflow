10 / FAQ

Everything you need to know about Payflow.

How quickly can we integrate Payflow? +

Most developer teams integrate Payflow in fewer than 48 hours. Our REST API and drop-in UI components allow you to accept payments instantly with minimal code.

Which currencies and payment methods are supported? +

Payflow supports 135+ currencies and all major payment methods including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, SEPA, ACH, and regional bank rails.

How does payout settlement work? +

Settlement occurs on a rolling T+1 or instant schedule depending on your account tier. Funds are transferred directly to your commercial bank account via local clearing systems.

Can Payflow handle subscriptions and complex billing schedules? +

Yes. Payflow Billing supports recurring subscriptions, usage-based metering, tiered pricing, dunning recovery, and automated tax calculation natively.

What happens when payments fail or get declined? +

Our intelligent routing engine retries declined transactions in real-time across alternate acquiring banks and updates expired cards automatically through network tokens.

How does fraud protection work? +

Payflow Radar evaluates every transaction against billions of global signals using hardware-accelerated ML models, stopping fraudulent attempts without impacting legitimate customers.
